WHO WE ARE

About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on 23 September 2020. Based in Chhattisgarh, the foundation works to create positive social change through community initiatives, education, welfare and sustainable development.

Its mission is to bring people together to build a compassionate, inclusive and empowered society.
